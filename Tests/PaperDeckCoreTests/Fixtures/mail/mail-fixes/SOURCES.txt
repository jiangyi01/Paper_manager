Synthetic fixtures for the MailFixes suites (generated by a script; no real mail).

outlook-export.eml  The shape a correct Power Automate "Export email (V2)" file has: RFC 822 with CRLF
                    line endings, Received-first headers, ARC-* / Authentication-Results /
                    X-MS-Exchange-* headers, multipart/mixed -> multipart/alternative (text/plain +
                    text/html, quoted-printable with soft line breaks) + an application/pdf attachment
                    (base64, Content-Disposition: attachment). Links are wrapped by Outlook Safe Links
                    (nam10.safelinks.protection.outlook.com) and the external-sender banner links aka.ms.
                    Names, addresses, ids, DOIs and the one-page PDF are made up.
html-only.eml       What a misconfigured flow writes when "Create file" gets the trigger's Body instead
                    of Export email's: HTML only (starts with "<html><head><meta"), CRLF, no mail headers.
scholar-alert.eml   Copy of Fixtures/mail/mail-parse/scholar-alert.eml (synthetic Google Scholar alert) so
                    these suites don't depend on another suite's fixture folder.
