# Installing PaperDeck

*Someone sent you PaperDeck. These steps take about two minutes. 中文见下方。*

1. **Unzip** `PaperDeck-….zip` (double-click it). You get **PaperDeck.app**.
2. **Move PaperDeck.app into your Applications folder** *before* opening it the first time. (Opened
   from Downloads, macOS runs it from a temporary copy where the widget and Open at Login don't work.)
3. **Open it.** macOS says it can't check the app for malicious software, because it doesn't come
   from the App Store or a registered developer. Click **Done** (or Cancel).
4. Open **System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security**, scroll down to the message about PaperDeck and click
   **Open Anyway**, then confirm with your password or Touch ID. This is needed once.
5. PaperDeck opens its **setup window**: choose your language, research field, whether to use AI
   (your own Claude or ChatGPT subscription — or none), where your library lives, and the daily
   reminder. Mail is optional.

PaperDeck lives in the **menu bar** and on a thin **strip at the edge of your screen** — there's no
Dock icon. Help is under **Help ▸ PaperDeck Help**.

Your settings, library, AI sign-in and mail are your own: nothing of the sender's is in the app.
To check the download, compare `shasum -a 256 PaperDeck-….zip` with the `.sha256` file you received.

# 安装 PaperDeck

*有人把 PaperDeck 发给了你。大约两分钟即可完成。*

1. **解压** `PaperDeck-….zip`（双击），得到 **PaperDeck.app**。
2. 第一次打开**之前**，先把 **PaperDeck.app 移到「应用程序」文件夹**。（从「下载」中直接打开时，macOS 会从临时副本运行它，
   小组件和「登录时打开」都无法使用。）
3. **打开它。** macOS 会提示无法检查这个应用是否含有恶意软件，因为它不是来自 App Store 或已注册的开发者。点「完成」（或「取消」）。
4. 打开「**系统设置 ▸ 隐私与安全性**」，向下滚动到关于 PaperDeck 的提示，点「**仍要打开**」，再用密码或触控 ID 确认。只需要一次。
5. PaperDeck 会打开**设置窗口**：选择语言、研究领域、是否使用 AI（你自己的 Claude 或 ChatGPT 订阅——也可以不用）、论文库的位置
   和每日提醒。邮件是可选的。

PaperDeck 住在**菜单栏**和**屏幕边缘的细竖条**上——没有 Dock 图标。帮助在「**帮助 ▸ PaperDeck 帮助**」中。

你的设置、论文库、AI 登录和邮件都属于你自己：应用里没有发送者的任何个人内容。
如需校验下载的文件，可以把 `shasum -a 256 PaperDeck-….zip` 的结果与收到的 `.sha256` 文件对比。
